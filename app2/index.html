Section 01

Introduction

La-Fierce is a community-first social networking application for Android. It is designed around positivity, self-expression, and genuine human connection. The name "La-Fierce" reflects the boldness and strength the platform encourages in each of its members — whether you're sharing your daily thoughts, reacting to friends' content, nominating someone who inspires you, or celebrating your own progress streak.

Unlike many social platforms that focus purely on follower counts and vanity metrics, La-Fierce places emphasis on authentic interaction: comments that uplift, nominations that recognise good people, and badges that reward consistent participation. The platform's AI-assisted comment moderation actively filters out negativity to keep the community warm and welcoming.

This manual covers every feature of the La-Fierce Android app in detail, from creating your account and navigating the home feed to using advanced features such as direct messaging, nominations, streaks, and reporting. Whether you are a first-time user or someone looking to make the most of what the platform offers, this guide has you covered.

Section 03

Account Setup

La-Fierce gives you two ways to create an account and sign in: standard registration with a username and password, and quick sign-in via your Google account. Both paths are equally supported, and you can use either method to access the same community.

3.1 Creating a New Account

To register for the first time, open the app and tap Sign Up on the Login screen. You will be taken to the registration form.

1
Enter a Username Choose a unique username that identifies you in the community. Usernames are case-insensitive and must not already be taken.
2
Enter your Email Address Provide a valid email address. This may be used for account recovery and Google Sign-In linking.
3
Create a Password Your password must be at least 6 characters long. Enter it twice to confirm and avoid typos.
4
Select Your Gender Choose Male, Female, or Other from the dropdown. This is used for personalising the experience (e.g., comment hints).
5
Tap Sign Up If all fields are valid, your account will be created and you will be redirected back to the login screen.
✅

If you see the message "Username already taken", that username is already in use. Try a variation, such as adding numbers or underscores, to find a unique one.

3.2 Signing In with Google

On the Sign Up screen, tap Sign In with Google. The Google account picker will appear. Select your Google account and the app will automatically create or log into an existing La-Fierce account linked to that email. No password is required for this method.

3.3 Logging In to an Existing Account

If you already have an account, enter your username and password on the Login screen and tap Login. The app performs a session check on launch — if you were previously logged in and your session token is still valid, you will be taken directly to the home feed without needing to log in again.

3.4 Session Management

La-Fierce stores your session token securely on your device. This means you remain logged in between app restarts until you explicitly log out. The app also refreshes your profile data (username, profile picture, follower counts) automatically each time you open the home screen, ensuring your displayed information is always current.

⚠️

If you receive an "incorrect username or password" error, double-check that Caps Lock is not active and that you are using the same username (not email) you registered with. If you enter incorrect credentials too many times, the server may temporarily rate-limit login attempts — wait one minute before trying again.

The feed shows all posts from all users, displayed in reverse-chronological order so the freshest content is always at the top. Pull down on the list at any time to refresh and load the latest posts.

At the top of the Posts feed, you will find a text input card. Type your message in the field and tap the Post button to share it with the entire community. There is no character limit restriction visible on the client, so feel free to express yourself fully. The app will confirm your post with an encouraging message such as "That's the fierce spirit we love! 🔥" or "You inspire us every time! 💛".

Each post card in the feed displays the author's avatar, username, and timestamp. Tapping the avatar of another user navigates to their profile. The following actions are available on every post:

Tap anywhere on the post card (not on a button) to open the Post Detail view. This full-screen view shows the post content, all comments in a dedicated list, the full list of likes, and any shares of that post. It gives you a complete picture of the engagement around a particular post.

5.4 Comment Moderation

La-Fierce uses an AI moderation layer to analyse comments before they are published. If the content of your comment is detected as negative or harmful, the server will reject it and display a gentle message encouraging you to reframe your words positively — for example: "Let's keep it kind and supportive here 💛" or "Fierce means strong AND kind! 💪". There is no penalty for a rejected comment; simply try again with a different tone.

5.5 Reporting a Post

If you see a post that violates community guidelines, you can report it by long-pressing on the post card. A report sheet will slide up from the bottom of the screen allowing you to select a reason (Spam, Hate speech, Harassment, False information, Violence, or Other) and optionally add a description. See Section 15 – Reporting for full details.

🔔

When someone likes or comments on your post, you will receive a push notification (if enabled) and the notification will also appear in the Notifications tab with a red badge count.

Section 06

Shares

The Shares tab shows all posts that community members have re-shared, with the original post displayed as a quoted block inside the share. A share can include an optional personal commentary written by the person sharing, giving it additional context or a personal perspective. The Shares feed works similarly to the Posts feed — it can be refreshed by pulling down and each share card has its own set of actions.

6.1 Creating a Share

To share someone else's post, tap the 🔁 Share button on any post card in the Posts feed. A bottom sheet dialog will appear allowing you to add an optional message (a quote or commentary) before publishing the share. You can also share without adding any text. Once shared, the item appears in the Shares feed with a quoted block showing the original post below your commentary.

6.2 Interactions on Shares

Each share card supports the same core interactions as posts: you can like a share, comment on a share (with the same AI positivity moderation), and tap the share to open it in the full Share Detail view. If you are the author of a share, an edit button appears allowing you to modify your commentary text or delete the share entirely.

6.3 Share Detail View

Tapping on a share card opens the Share Detail Activity, which displays the share's full content and all its comments in one scrollable screen. The original quoted post is shown within the detail view so the full context is always clear.

💡

Sharing someone's post is a great way to amplify content you find inspiring or important. The original post author will be notified when their post is shared, helping to celebrate the content they created.

Section 07

Search

The Search tab lets you find people, posts, and hashtags across the entire La-Fierce community. Results appear as a combined list grouped by category: users first, then posts, then hashtags.

7.1 Using the Search Bar

Type at least 2 characters in the search bar to begin a search. Results are fetched automatically after a short debounce delay (approximately 400 ms), so you do not need to press a button — results appear as you type. Clear the field to return to the trending hashtags view.

7.2 Trending Hashtags

Before you begin typing, the Search screen shows a row of trending hashtag chips. Tap any chip to instantly populate the search bar with that hashtag's text and see related posts immediately. Trending topics are updated server-side and reflect the most popular discussions happening right now in the community.

7.3 Search Result Types

👤

User Results

Shows matching community members with their avatar, username, and verification badge. Tap a user to go to their profile.

📝

Post Results

Shows posts whose content matches your search term. Tap a post result to navigate to the full Post Detail screen.

#️⃣

Hashtag Results

Shows hashtags related to your query with their usage count. Tap a hashtag to see all posts tagged with it.

✅

Verified Badges

Verified community members are marked with a ✅ checkmark in search results so you can easily identify trusted accounts.

💡

The Search tab doubles as a user directory when starting a new conversation. In the New Conversation dialog (Messages tab), the same search logic is used to find users you want to message.

Search for a user by typing at least 2 characters of their username, then tap their name in the results list. If no previous conversation exists between you and that user, one will be created automatically — you will be taken directly to the chat screen.

9.2 Inside a Chat

The chat screen shows the conversation history in a speech-bubble layout: your messages appear on the right with a distinct bubble style, and messages from the other person appear on the left. Timestamps are shown for each message. Type your message in the input field at the bottom and tap Send to deliver it.

Section 10

Follow & Friends

La-Fierce features two distinct social connection systems: a public follow relationship (one-directional, like most social networks) and a mutual friend request system (two-directional, requiring acceptance). Both are managed from user profile pages.

10.1 Following a User

Navigate to any user's profile and tap Follow. The button label changes to "Following" and the user's follower count increments immediately. Tap it again to unfollow. Following does not require the other person's approval — it is a one-way subscription to their content.

10.2 Follower & Following Lists

On your own Profile page, tap the Followers or Following count to open a full list of the respective users. You can tap any user in those lists to visit their profile.

10.3 Friend Requests

Sending a friend request is a way to establish a closer mutual connection. On any user's profile, tap Add Friend. The button changes to Pending once the request is sent. The receiving user will be notified and can accept or decline. Once accepted, the button displays Friends ✓. You can cancel a pending request by tapping the button again before it is accepted.

Button StateMeaningAction When Tapped
Add Friend No relationship exists yet Sends a friend request to the user
Pending Your request is awaiting approval Cancels and withdraws the friend request
Respond They sent you a request Opens options to Accept or Decline
Friends ✓ Mutual friends Tap to unfriend (removes mutual connection)

10.4 Managing Incoming Friend Requests

Open the Navigation Drawer → Friend Requests to see a full list of pending incoming requests. Each request shows the sender's avatar and username. Tap Accept to confirm the friendship or Decline to reject it. Accepting a request establishes a mutual friendship visible on both profiles.

12.1 Posting Streaks

A streak tracks how many consecutive days you have posted. At the top of the Badges screen you will see:

🔥 Current Streak: X days
🏆 Best Streak: Y days

The current streak increments by one each day you publish at least one post. Miss a day and your streak resets to zero. Your longest streak (personal best) is preserved permanently even if your current streak resets, serving as a motivational benchmark.

Badges are awarded by the platform when you reach specific milestones. Each badge has an emoji icon, a name, and a description explaining what you did to earn it. Badges are displayed in a 3-column grid so you can see at a glance which achievements you have unlocked. Section 14

Bookmarks

Bookmarks allow you to save any post you want to revisit later. This is useful for inspiring posts, important announcements, or any content you want to keep at your fingertips without searching for it again.

14.1 Saving a Post

On any post card, tap the 🔖 Save button. The button immediately changes to "Saved" and the icon turns orange — this is an optimistic UI update that gives you instant visual feedback while the save is being confirmed with the server in the background. A toast message confirms "Post saved 🔖".

14.2 Removing a Bookmark

Tap the "Saved" button (now orange) on a previously bookmarked post to remove it. The button reverts to the default "Save" state and the post is removed from your saved list. A toast message confirms "Bookmark removed".

14.3 Reliability

The bookmark system uses an optimistic UI approach: the visual change happens instantly on your screen without waiting for the server response. If the network request fails (for example, due to a connection issue), the UI automatically reverts to its previous state and displays "Failed to update bookmark 😕", so you always know what's actually saved.

💡

Your bookmarks are stored server-side and are therefore preserved across devices and app reinstalls. Log in on a different Android device with the same account and your saved posts will still be there.

Section 15

Reporting Content

La-Fierce is committed to maintaining a safe and positive environment. The reporting system gives every member the power to flag content that violates community guidelines. Reports are reviewed by the La-Fierce moderation team.

15.1 How to Report a Post

Long-press (press and hold) on any post card in the feed. A bottom sheet report dialog will appear. Select the most appropriate reason from the dropdown, optionally add a brief description to provide context, and tap Submit Report. A confirmation toast will thank you for helping keep La-Fierce safe.

Section 16

Editing Your Profile

You can update your personal details at any time from the Navigation Drawer → Edit Profile. The Edit Profile screen lets you change your username, gender, password, and profile photo.

16.1 Changing Your Username

Edit the username field with your new desired name and tap Update Profile. If the username is already taken by another user, the server will reject it. Your new username will be saved to your local session immediately upon success and will appear throughout the app (navigation drawer, posts, comments, etc.).

16.2 Changing Your Gender

Select a new option from the Gender dropdown (Male, Female, or Other) and tap Update Profile. This updates how other users' comment hints are personalised when writing on your posts.

16.3 Changing Your Password

Enter your new password in the "New Password" field and confirm it in "Confirm Password". Both must match and be at least 6 characters. Tap Change Password to save. For security, the old password is not required in the current version — ensure you are in a trusted environment when changing passwords.

16.4 Updating Your Profile Picture

Tap your profile photo (or the photo container area) to open the system image picker. Select an image from your gallery or file manager. The app will then perform an important step: it uses Google ML Kit Face Detection to verify that the selected image contains a human face before uploading. This ensures all profile pictures are genuine portraits.

1
Tap your profile picture The image selector opens. Browse to your desired photo.
2
Select the photo The app processes the image through face detection. A clear, front-facing portrait works best.
3
Face detected — upload proceeds If a face is found, the image is converted to Base64 and uploaded to the server.
4
Profile picture updated Your new photo appears immediately in the Edit Profile screen, navigation drawer, and throughout the app.
📌

If no face is detected in the selected image, the upload will be blocked and you will be prompted to select a photo that clearly shows a face. Group photos may work but individual portraits give the most reliable detection result.

Section 17

Theme & Display Settings

La-Fierce supports both a Light Mode and a Dark Mode. Dark Mode uses a deep black background with orange accents, which is easier on the eyes in low-light environments and conserves battery on OLED screens. Light Mode uses a clean white interface.

17.1 Switching Themes

Open the Navigation Drawer and tap Switch Theme. The theme toggles immediately throughout the entire app — all screens, dialogs, and input fields adapt to the selected theme. Your preference is saved locally and persists across app restarts.

17.2 System Bars

La-Fierce enforces a black status bar and navigation bar regardless of theme to maintain the brand's visual identity. This is applied consistently across all screens and is re-enforced each time the app resumes from the background.

🌙

Dark Mode

Deep black backgrounds with orange accent colour. Ideal for night-time use, reduces eye strain and saves battery on OLED displays.

☀️

Clean white backgrounds with bold dark text. Perfect for daytime use in bright environments with natural lighting.

Section 20

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Why was my comment rejected?

La-Fierce uses AI moderation to maintain a positive community atmosphere. If your comment contains language that is detected as negative, harmful, or hurtful, it will be blocked before reaching the server and you will see a gentle suggestion to try again with a more supportive tone. This is not a ban or penalty — simply revise your wording and submit again. Comments that are constructive, encouraging, or empathetic are always welcome.

I'm not receiving push notifications — what's wrong?

First, check that notification permission is granted: go to your device's Settings → Apps → La-Fierce → Notifications and ensure it is toggled on. On Android 13 and above, this permission must be explicitly granted. If the permission is already on but you still aren't receiving notifications, try logging out and back in — this triggers a fresh FCM token registration with the server.

How do I delete a post I've made?

Tap the ✏️ Edit button visible on your own post cards. In the edit dialog, you will find both an option to modify the content and an option to delete the post entirely. Deleted posts are removed from the feed immediately.

Why can't I upload my profile picture?

La-Fierce requires your profile picture to contain a clearly visible human face, verified via Google ML Kit on-device face detection. If the image you selected does not contain a detectable face, the upload will be blocked. Try selecting a clear, well-lit portrait photo where your face is prominently visible and facing forward. Group photos may work but individual portraits are more reliable.

My streak reset — can it be restored?

Streaks cannot be manually restored. A streak resets when you go a full calendar day without publishing at least one post. The best strategy is to post daily, even something brief, to maintain your streak. Your all-time longest streak is preserved permanently and cannot be lost, so that record always stands.

How do I find someone I know on La-Fierce?

Use the Search tab and type their username. If you know at least 2 characters of their name, the search will find them. You can also browse follower and following lists on profiles you already know, as mutual connections often appear there.

Why does the app show "Looks like the server is taking a nap"?

This friendly message appears when the app cannot connect to the La-Fierce server. This can happen due to an internet outage on your end, a temporary server maintenance window, or slow network conditions. Check your Wi-Fi or mobile data connection and try again. Pulling down to refresh the feed will attempt a fresh connection.

I sent a friend request by mistake — can I cancel it?

Yes. Navigate to the profile of the person you accidentally sent a request to, and tap the Pending button. This will immediately cancel and withdraw your friend request before the other person accepts it.

Is my data private?

Posts and shares are visible to all community members. Direct messages are private between you and the recipient. Your profile picture and username are publicly visible. Your email address is stored on the server but not displayed to other users. For any privacy concerns, use the Feedback tab to contact the La-Fierce team directly.
